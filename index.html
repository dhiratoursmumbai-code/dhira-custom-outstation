Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful information before you send your enquiry.

Do you provide Tempo Traveller rentals in Mumbai?

Yes. Dhira Tours & Travels handles Tempo Traveller enquiries for Mumbai local and outstation group travel, subject to vehicle availability.

Which seating options can I enquire about?

You can enquire for 13, 17, 20 and 26 seater options. AC and Non-AC availability depends on the requested date and vehicle availability.

Can I book for a wedding or family trip?

Yes. The website accepts enquiries for weddings, family holidays, corporate trips, picnics, functions and outstation journeys.

How do I get the final price?

Share your pickup point, destination, travel date, passenger count and vehicle requirement. The final quote depends on the trip details, vehicle option and availability.

Can I directly WhatsApp my booking request?

Yes. Use any WhatsApp button on the website or submit the enquiry form. The form will generate a pre-filled WhatsApp message for you.
